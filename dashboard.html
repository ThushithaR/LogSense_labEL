Novel technique: Log-Aware Tokenization
Standard BERT tokenizers fragment structured log entities:

192.168.1.50 → ['192','.','168','.','1','.','50']

LogAwarePreTokenizer replaces with semantic placeholders:

192.168.1.50 → [IP_ADDR]
sshd[1234] → sshd [PID]
0x7f3a1b → [HEX_VAL]

Applied Cryptography — Encrypted Anomaly Store
Why only anomalies are stored

Normal log lines are discarded after classification. Only detected anomalies are preserved as forensic evidence. Storing everything encrypted would be wasteful and defeats the purpose of the detection layer.
AES-256-CTR + SHA-256 integrity

Each anomaly record is AES-256-CTR encrypted before being appended to anomalies.enc. The SHA-256 hash of the plaintext is stored alongside to verify tampering during forensic review. Click "View & Download" in the Live Monitor to decrypt and export.
